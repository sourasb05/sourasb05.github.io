About Me

I am currently an AI Application Expert at the Sweden AI Factory, hosted by NAISS, where I organize and deliver training, provide consultancy on AI projects, and build automation that helps researchers and organizations make the most of AI. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Information Technology, Uppsala University, and I received my Ph.D. in Computing Science from Umeå University.

My research spans federated learning, continual learning, IoT cyber security, image privacy, and autobiographical memory recall, with a focus on building AI systems that are private, adaptive, and trustworthy.

    Research Interests

    My research lies at the intersection of federated learning, privacy-preserving machine learning, and distributed AI systems, focusing on theoretically grounded and practically validated themes:

    ⚖

    Personalization & Heterogeneity

    Provably convergent FL algorithms that handle non-IID data and systems heterogeneity across large-scale distributed environments.

    ↻

    Continual Federated Learning

    Adapting federated models to distributional shifts without catastrophic forgetting, applied to adaptive intrusion detection in IoT networks.

    🛡

    Trustworthy Federated Learning

    Quantifying and mitigating privacy leakage and adversarial threats in FL pipelines, for verifiably secure learning across IoT and cross-institutional settings.

    🔍

    Explainable Federated Learning

    Attributing global model behavior to individual client contributions — enabling anomaly detection, fair valuation and accountability in high-stakes domains.

    🧠

    Applied Federated Learning

    User-centric FL for continuous sensing, including cognitive health, image privacy, and cross-institutional collaboration under data scarcity.

    📡

    IoT Security

    Intrusion detection for RPL-based IoT networks using LSTMs, foundation models and radio-layer features.
